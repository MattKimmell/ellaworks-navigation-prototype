How navigation works: the bottom-left switcher changes work area and sidebar. Build, Configure, Test, Monitor and Deploy are fixed headings. Monitor contains Logs and Usage; Deploy contains Rollouts. Most pages use contextual tabs. Team conversation uses a focused chat layout: Workspace opens product navigation; Team tools opens the existing team pages. Elladex stays above the switcher with “Discover and submit agents”; organization and account controls stay outside the page list.
